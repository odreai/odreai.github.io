Ansim365 Lite Privacy Policy

ODRE AI (the “Developer”) values the privacy of Ansim365 Lite users and publishes this Privacy Policy to transparently explain what information the app accesses and uses and how that information is handled.

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Ansim365 Lite does not require registration or login and does not transmit contacts or check-in settings entered by the user to the Developer’s own server.

1. Information Accessed or Processed by the App

Ansim365 Lite may process the following information on the device to provide its core features.

  • Registered family or acquaintance phone number: Entered directly by the user to send an emergency SMS when there is no response.
  • Check-in time and no-response threshold: Used to schedule alarms and determine whether there has been no response.
  • Check-in response status: Processed on the device to determine whether the user responded to the alarm.
  • Purchase status: Used to verify activation of lifetime access to automatic SMS sending.

Phone numbers, alarm settings, and response status are stored in the app’s local storage and are not transmitted to an external server operated by the Developer.

2. Purposes for Using Permissions

  • Notification permission: Used to display alarms and notifications at the configured check-in time.
  • Exact alarm permission: Used to run the check-in alarm precisely at the time specified by the user.
  • Full-screen intent permission: Used to display the alarm screen on the lock screen or while the screen is off.
  • Vibration permission: Used to provide vibration for the check-in alarm.
  • SMS permission: Used to automatically send an emergency SMS to the registered family member or acquaintance when the user does not respond to the check-in.
  • Boot completed receiver: Used to reschedule the user’s saved check-in alarm after the phone restarts.

SMS permission is requested with the user’s separate consent after lifetime access is purchased. Automatic SMS sending does not operate if the user does not grant permission.

3. Automatic SMS Sending

If the user does not respond to the configured check-in alarm within the specified time, the app may send an emergency SMS to the phone number registered in advance by the user.

The SMS is sent through the user’s phone and mobile network. Message charges may apply depending on the user’s mobile plan. The Developer does not collect or store the recipient phone number or SMS content on its own server.

4. Processing of Payment Information

Payment for Ansim365 Lite lifetime access is processed through the Google Play billing system. The Developer does not directly collect or retain the user’s actual payment method information, such as credit card or bank account numbers.

The app may use Google Play Billing to verify purchase status. Payment-related information may be handled under Google’s applicable terms and privacy policy.

5. Provision and Sharing of Personal Information with Third Parties

The Developer does not sell or provide family or acquaintance phone numbers, check-in times, or response status entered in the app to advertising companies, data brokers, or other third parties.

However, the user’s mobile network is used to perform SMS sending, and Google Play payment services are used to process in-app purchases.

6. Retention and Deletion

Phone numbers, alarm settings, and check-in status entered by the user are retained in the app’s on-device storage. No separate member account is created, and no user profile is stored on the Developer’s server.

The user may modify stored information through the app settings. Local information stored by the app can also be deleted by clearing the app’s storage in the phone settings or uninstalling the app.

7. Information Protection

The app is designed to process information only to the extent required for its core features. Phone numbers and check-in settings entered by the user are processed in app-specific local storage and, as a rule, are not transmitted to the Developer’s own server.

8. Use by Children and Adolescents

Ansim365 Lite is not a service intended only for a specific age group. If the user is a minor, a guardian should review the automatic SMS recipient, permission settings, and purpose of use together with the user.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may change if the app’s features, permissions, or personal information handling practices change. Material changes will be announced through the app or the published Privacy Policy page.
