JIGEUM ON · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Jigeum ON values your privacy and processes only the minimum information required to provide the service.

Our basic principle

Jigeum ON does not require sign-up or login and does not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, contacts, location data, photos, videos, or chat content.

1. Information we process

We may process the following minimum information to provide the service:

  • An internal device UUID generated by the app
  • Pair connection identifiers
  • Owner or Partner role
  • ON/OFF status
  • Status signals selected by the user
  • Connection codes and connection status
  • Minimum timestamps required to operate the service
  • Subscription entitlement and product identifiers

The internal UUID is a random identifier generated by the app to distinguish a device within Jigeum ON.

2. Information we do not collect

Jigeum ON does not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, contacts, GPS location, photos, videos, voice recordings, or chat and message content for its service features.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to connect two users in a private 1:1 Pair, share ON/OFF status and selected signals, maintain and verify the connection, verify subscription entitlement, and maintain service stability and respond to errors.

4. Connection codes

The 7-digit connection code is not an account ID that directly identifies a user. It is a temporary code for connecting two users and may become unusable after the connection is completed.

5. Retention and deletion

Information required for the service may be retained while a connection or service use continues. When you use Delete my data, the applicable service data and connection information stored on the Jigeum ON server are deleted through the required operational process. Information required by applicable law may be retained separately for the legally required period.

6. Google Play subscriptions

Paid service may use Google Play Billing. Payment information is processed under Google policies and systems. Jigeum ON does not store direct payment instrument information such as your credit card number. We may process minimum subscription status and product information to verify entitlement.

7. Sharing with third parties

Jigeum ON does not arbitrarily sell or provide personal information to third parties. Information may be processed when required by a lawful request or when necessary to provide the service, in accordance with applicable law and privacy standards.

8. Your choices

You may disconnect a Pair or use Delete my data in the app. Because Jigeum ON is not based on membership accounts, it provides Disconnect and Delete my data instead of conventional account withdrawal.

9. App deletion and subscriptions

Uninstalling the app, deleting app data, or deleting Jigeum ON server data does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop recurring billing, you must cancel the subscription separately through Google Play subscription management.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated due to changes in law, service features, or operating policy. Important changes may be announced in the app or on a service page.

11. Contact

For privacy or Jigeum ON service questions, contact odreai2025@gmail.com.
