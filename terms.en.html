Jigeum ON

A private 1:1 status-sharing service that lets two connected people check each other’s current availability without sending messages.

1. Purpose of the service

Jigeum ON lets two connected users share ON/OFF status and selected signals. It is not a chat, telephone, location-tracking, or emergency communication service.

2. Using the service

Jigeum ON can be used without sign-up or login. It distinguishes devices and maintains connections using an internal UUID generated by the app. Existing connections may become unavailable after app removal, app-data deletion, or a device change.

3. Pair connections

The service normally connects two users as one Pair. The user who creates a connection code becomes the Owner, and the user who enters it becomes the Partner. One active Pair can contain only one Owner and one Partner.

Users deliver connection codes directly to the intended person and must share them only with a trusted person. Users should confirm the intended partner to prevent an incorrect connection caused by disclosure to a third party.
